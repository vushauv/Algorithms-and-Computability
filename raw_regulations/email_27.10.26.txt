Subject: AaC, output format

Dear All,
Below are the requirements for the output file of the solution.
Solution output format:
Results must be saved to disk. The output file name is the input file name with _output.txt in place of.txt: AaC_example_1.txt becomes AaC_example_1_output.txt.
First line: n (number of sets) and k (number of subsets, given to the program as a parameter), separated by a space.

* For each set, a first line: the sum of the whole set, a space, then the original set in input order, with numbers separated by a comma and a space.
* Then k lines, one per subset: the subset's sum, a space, then its numbers separated by a comma and a space.
* Then maximal difference of sums of numbers in subsets

A blank line separates consecutive sets.
Two sorting rules apply:
Within a subset: numbers in non-increasing order (largest first).
Between subsets: lines ordered lexicographically non-increasing by their content: compare the largest numbers first, then the second largest, and so on. Subsets are not ordered by their sums.
Example of input and output files is attached.
