## 7. References

- M. Fischetti and S. Martello, "Worst-case analysis of the differencing method for the partition problem", *Mathematical Programming* 37(1), 117–120, 1987. Cited in Section 5.5 for the worst-case ratio $7/6$ of the differencing method.
- M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness*, W. H. Freeman, 1979. Cited in Sections 1.1 and 1.3 for pseudo-polynomial algorithms and strong NP-completeness (Section 4.2 of the book).
- R. L. Graham, "Bounds on multiprocessing timing anomalies", *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* 17(2), 416–429, 1969. Cited in Section 5.7 for the ratio of the greedy method (longest processing time first).
- E. Horowitz and S. Sahni, "Computing partitions with applications to the knapsack problem", *Journal of the ACM* 21(2), 277–292, 1974. Cited in Sections 1.1 and 3 as the origin of meet in the middle.
- N. Karmarkar and R. M. Karp, "The differencing method of set partitioning", Technical Report UCB/CSD-83-113, Computer Science Division, University of California, Berkeley, 1983. Cited in Sections 1.1 and 5 as the origin of the differencing method.
- R. M. Karp, "Reducibility among combinatorial problems", in R. E. Miller and J. W. Thatcher (eds.), *Complexity of Computer Computations*, Plenum Press, 85–103, 1972. Cited in Sections 1.1 and 1.3 for the NP-completeness of PARTITION.
- R. E. Korf, "A complete anytime algorithm for number partitioning", *Artificial Intelligence* 106(2), 181–203, 1998. Cited in Section 5.7 for the exact search built on the differencing method.
- R. Schroeppel and A. Shamir, "A $T = O(2^{n/2})$, $S = O(2^{n/4})$ algorithm for certain NP-complete problems", *SIAM Journal on Computing* 10(3), 456–464, 1981. Cited in Section 3.6 for the reduction of the memory of meet in the middle.
- B. Yakir, "The differencing algorithm LDM for partitioning: a proof of a conjecture of Karmarkar and Karp", *Mathematics of Operations Research* 21(1), 85–99, 1996. Cited in Sections 1.1 and 5.5 for the expected discrepancy $n^{-\Theta(\log n)}$ on i.i.d. uniform items.
